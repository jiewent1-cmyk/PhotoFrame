Privacy Policy

Encadré for iPhone, iPad and Mac · Effective 28 October 2026

Encadré does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising. Every photo is processed on your own device.

No data is collected

Encadré has no account system. Nothing you open, import, or export is sent anywhere. Your photos never leave the device, so there is nothing for anyone — including us — to receive. The app's only own network request downloads public camera data, as described below; it uploads nothing.

Photos stay on your device

Photos are read only after you choose them: from the Photos library, from the Files app, or through the share sheet from the Photos app. The border is built entirely on your device, from the file you selected.

When you export, the result is written to the destination you picked — your Photos library, the Files app, or another app you share it to. Photos you did not select are never accessed.

On Mac, Encadré reads only the photos and folders you open or drag in, and writes only to the export folder you choose.

Photos permission

The Photos permission is requested at the moment it is needed, and is used for two things only:

  • Reading the specific photos you select, so they can be framed.
  • Writing the results you export, including creating an album named Encadré when you choose that option.

Location information

Some photos carry GPS coordinates in their EXIF metadata. That information belongs to the photo, not to the app: Encadré never asks for your device's location and never reads it.

Before exporting you can choose a Privacy level: Keep all, Remove location (GPS and location tags), or Remove location and identity (also the camera serial number, owner name and similar fields). It applies only to the copy you export and leaves the original untouched. The default is Keep all.

Settings stored on your device

Encadré stores your preferences — border, colour, watermark and export choices — and the presets you save on your device. We never receive them.

iCloud sync of presets

If Sync with iCloud is on (you can turn it off in the Presets screen), your presets and camera profiles are kept in sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, in your own iCloud account. Only preset settings are synced — never photos. This data is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we cannot access it.

Camera data updates

Starting with version 1.4, Encadré may check for an updated camera database when it launches, by downloading public data files (camera models, brand logos, platform sizes) from the Encadré website at jiewent1-cmyk.github.io/PhotoFrame/data/. These are plain downloads: no photo, setting, identifier or usage information is sent. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; as with any web page, GitHub receives standard connection information such as your IP address, which we do not receive or use. You can turn this off with Update camera data automatically (iPhone and iPad: the About screen; Mac: the app menu). When it is off, the app makes no network requests of its own.

No third-party SDKs

Encadré contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no tracking SDK, and integrates no third-party services. Besides downloading the public camera data described above, the only service it uses is Apple's iCloud, for the optional preset sync.

Children's privacy

Encadré collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. Because nothing is collected, there is no information that could identify a user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on GitHub:

https://github.com/jiewent1-cmyk/PhotoFrame/issues

隐私政策

Encadré（iPhone、iPad 与 Mac） · 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日

Encadré 不收集任何个人数据，不在服务器上存储，也不与任何人分享。没有账号，没有统计分析，没有广告。所有照片都在你自己的设备上处理。

不收集任何数据

Encadré 没有账号体系。你打开、导入或导出的内容不会被发送到任何地方。照片不会离开设备，因此没有任何数据会被传送到我们这里。App 自身唯一的网络请求是下载公开的机型数据（见下文），不上传任何内容。

照片只在设备上处理

照片只在你选择之后才会被读取：从「照片」相册、从「文件」App，或从「照片」App 的分享菜单进来。边框完全在你的设备上，基于你选中的那个文件生成。

导出时，结果只会写入你选择的目标：你的「照片」相册、「文件」App，或你分享到的其它 App。你没有选中的照片，App 永远不会访问。

在 Mac 上，Encadré 只读取你打开或拖入的照片和文件夹，只写入你选择的导出文件夹。

相册权限

相册权限只在需要的那一刻才请求，并且只用于两件事：

  • 读取你选中的那几张照片，用来加边框。
  • 写入你导出的结果；如果你打开了相应选项，还会创建一个名为「Encadré」的相簿。

位置信息

有些照片的 EXIF 数据里带有 GPS 坐标。那是照片本身携带的信息，不是 App 获取的：Encadré 从不请求、也从不读取你设备的位置。

导出前可以选择隐私级别：全部保留、去除位置（GPS 与位置相关标签），或去除位置与身份信息（另去掉相机序列号、机主姓名等字段）。它只作用于导出的副本，原图不会被动。默认为全部保留。

保存在设备上的设置

Encadré 会把你的偏好——边框、颜色、水印和导出选项——以及你存的预设保存在设备上。我们不会收到这些数据。

预设的 iCloud 同步

打开通过 iCloud 同步后（可在「预设」界面关闭），你的预设和相机档案会通过苹果的 iCloud 键值存储，在你自己的 iCloud 账户里于 iPhone、iPad 与 Mac 之间同步。同步的只有预设设置，从不包括照片。这些数据由苹果按其隐私政策处理，我们无法访问。

机型库更新

从 1.4 版起，Encadré 启动时可能会检查机型库更新：从 Encadré 官网 jiewent1-cmyk.github.io/PhotoFrame/data/ 下载公开的数据文件（相机机型、品牌 Logo、平台尺寸）。这只是单纯的下载，不发送任何照片、设置、标识符或使用情况。官网托管在 GitHub Pages 上，和访问任何网页一样，GitHub 会收到 IP 地址等标准连接信息，我们收不到也不会使用。你可以关闭自动更新机型库（iPhone 和 iPad 在「关于」页，Mac 在 App 菜单里）。关闭后，App 自身不再发起任何网络请求。

没有第三方 SDK

Encadré 不包含统计分析 SDK、广告 SDK 或追踪 SDK，也没有接入任何第三方服务。除了上面所说的下载公开机型数据，唯一用到的服务是苹果的 iCloud，即可选的预设同步。

儿童隐私

Encadré 不收集任何人的数据，13 岁以下的儿童同样如此。既然什么都不收集，也就不存在任何可以识别用户的信息。

政策变更

如果本政策发生变化，更新后的版本会发布在这个页面上，并标注新的生效日期。

联系方式

关于本政策的问题，可以在 GitHub 上提交 issue：
